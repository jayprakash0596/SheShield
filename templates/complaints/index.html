⚠️ Important Legal & Administrative Notice

Preparing or saving a draft inside SheShield is not equivalent to filing a formal complaint or FIR with a government authority. SheShield assists you in organizing your narrative clearly for your own records. You must submit your finalized document directly to statutory bodies (like NCW, SHe-Box, or Police) as guided in Step 7.
